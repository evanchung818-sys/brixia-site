Why we're building this

Planning a wedding shouldn't feel like running a call center.

We've been talking with couples at the New York City Marriage Bureau. The same story keeps coming up.

Vendors everywhere

Quotes in email, updates by text, a voicemail from a number you don't recognize. Nothing lives in one place.

Who said what?

Was it the second caterer or the third who could do a tasting in November? Good luck finding that thread.

Left on delivered

Every unanswered message is one more thing to remember. It's easy to lose track of a vendor you actually loved.

How it works

One folder for the whole wedding

  1. 1

    Create your wedding folder

    Add your date, city, guest count and budget. Brixia uses them to find vendors that actually fit.

  2. 2

    Browse by category

    Venues, photographers, florists and more, filtered to the ones open on your date. Save your favorites and compare them side by side.

  3. 3

    Message everyone in one inbox

    Chats are grouped by vendor type, quotes sit next to each other, and you check a category off the moment you book.
